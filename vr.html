Mouse: drag to turn, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Click a cell name to pick it.
In VR: trigger = pick a cell · grip = grab and move · both grips = resize · right stick = turn / zoom · left stick = tilt, flick sideways = next cell · A = see-through · B = reset · X = only the picked cell · Y = menu (layers, vessels, EM section, cutting plane, size, cells, exit VR). With the cut in your hand, the right trigger freezes it in place and frees it again. With Measure on, the right trigger (or a mouse click) sets the two ends of a measurement.
