Mouse: drag to turn, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan, right-click to fly to a point. Keys: W A S D walk, Q E down / up. Click a cell name to pick it.
In VR: trigger = pick a cell · grip = grab and move · both grips = resize · right stick = turn / zoom · left stick = tilt, flick sideways = next cell · A = see-through · B = reset · X = only the picked cell · press the right stick in = fly to where it points · Y = palette on your left hand (tabs Show · Map · Tools · Spray · EM · Cells · View; point the right controller and pull the trigger). With the cut in your hand, the right trigger freezes it in place and frees it again. To film in the headset, use its own recorder (Quest: Meta button → Camera → Record video); the movie lands in the headset's gallery. With Measure on, the right trigger (or a mouse click) sets the two ends of a measurement.
