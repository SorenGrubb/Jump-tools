🔎 Check others' finds

When somebody corrects the AI or finds an organelle it cannot learn yet, their points wait until somebody else agrees. You look at the spot blind — without being told what they called it — and say what you see. For people who know the tissue well; "Can't tell" is always fine. 1 point for every 5 answers. Needs your Licence to Jump.

🪙 How the coins work

  • On the tracing pad, with the AI's suggestions on, check its marks: right, wrong, or what it really is. A gold coin sometimes jumps out — often for rare organelles (centrioles, cilia), seldom for mitochondria or chromatin.
  • The same spot again earns nothing, and many answers in one small area earn less: move around the cell.
  • Correcting the AI earns 2 points, and finding a type it cannot learn yet earns 10, once somebody else confirms it.
  • Now and then a mark or a find has a known answer. Your accuracy on those is shown in your stats; below 70% the coins stop until it comes back up. Practice here is never scored that way.
